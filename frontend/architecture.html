1. Sub-Second Acoustic DSP & Voice Deepfake Forensics

200ms Audio Chunks @ 16 kHz • Phase, Jitter, Centroid Stability & SNR Guard

VOICE SHIELD ENGINE

1.1 High-Frequency (8–16 kHz) STFT Phase Variance

VOCODER DETECTION
$$\Delta \phi(f, t) = \text{Var}_t \left( \arg(Z(f, t)) \right) \quad \text{where} \quad Z(f, t) = \text{STFT}(y)[f \ge 8\text{kHz}, t]$$ $$\text{Risk}_{\text{phase}} = 1.0 - \min\left(1.0, \ \frac{\overline{\Delta \phi}}{\beta_{\text{norm}}}\right) \quad (\beta_{\text{norm}} = 3.0\text{ rad}^2)$$
Where As:
• $y(t)$: $16\text{ kHz}$ raw PCM audio stream ($200\text{ms}$ chunk $= 3200$ samples ingested in volatile TEE RAM).
• $Z(f, t)$: Complex Short-Time Fourier Transform with $N_{\text{FFT}}=512$ bins, hop length $H=128$.
• $\arg(Z(f, t))$: Instantaneous spectral phase angle computed via $\text{atan2}(\text{Im}(Z), \text{Re}(Z))$.
• $f \ge 8\text{ kHz}$: Upper harmonic band where neural vocoders synthesize unnaturally smooth phase angles.
• $\beta_{\text{norm}} = 3.0\text{ rad}^2$: Empirical upper bound for natural human turbulent phase variance.
💡 Kyu & Kiske Liye (Why & Purpose):
Neural Vocoder Artifacts (HiFi-GAN, WaveGlow, FastSpeech) pakadne ke liye. Asli insani bolchal me vocal tract ki turbulent airflow ki wajah se high frequency ($8-16\text{ kHz}$) me phase random aur chaotic ($\Delta \phi \ge 2.5$) hota hai. AI models phase ko mathematically smooth aur flat generate karte hain, isliye low phase variance $\implies$ High AI Risk Alert.

1.2 Pitch Micro-Jitter (Fundamental Frequency $F_0$ Perturbation via pYIN)

LARYNGEAL BIOMECHANICS
$$\text{Jitter} = \frac{\frac{1}{N-1}\sum_{i=1}^{N-1} |F_0(i+1) - F_0(i)|}{\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} F_0(i)} \implies \text{Risk}_{\text{jitter}} = 1.0 - \min\left(1.0, \frac{\text{Jitter}}{0.05}\right)$$
Where As:
• $F_0(i)$: Fundamental pitch frequency per voiced frame via probabilistic YIN (pYIN) in range $65\text{ Hz} - 1047\text{ Hz}$ ($C_2 \to C_6$).
• $N$: Total count of active voiced speech frames in the $200\text{ms}$ window.
• $0.05$ ($5\%$): Upper physiological boundary for natural vocal cord micro-perturbation.
💡 Kyu & Kiske Liye (Why & Purpose):
Laryngeal Biomechanical Inconsistency Check (Robotic Pitch Fluctuation). Insani gale ke vocal folds ke elastic muscle vibration se cycle-to-cycle $1.5\% - 4.5\%$ pitch jitter naturally hota hai. AI text-to-speech models mathematically pure, robotic aur super-steady pitch generate karte hain jisme jitter almost zero hota hai.

1.3 Spectral Centroid Dynamic Variance & Timbre Stability

FORMANT DYNAMICS
$$\mu_c(t) = \frac{\sum_k f_k \cdot |X_t(f_k)|}{\sum_k |X_t(f_k)|}, \quad \sigma_c = \text{std}_t(\mu_c) \implies \text{Risk}_{\text{centroid}} = 1.0 - \min\left(1.0, \frac{\sigma_c}{400\text{ Hz}}\right)$$
Where As:
• $f_k$: Center frequency of $k$-th FFT bin ($k \cdot f_s / N_{\text{FFT}}$); $|X_t(f_k)|$: Spectral magnitude energy.
• $\mu_c(t)$: Spectral center of gravity ("vocal brightness"); $\sigma_c$: Standard deviation across frames.
• $400\text{ Hz}$: Dynamic formant transition threshold; neural clones exhibit flat timbre dynamics.
💡 Kyu & Kiske Liye (Why & Purpose):
Synthetic Formant Flatness Check (Timbre Dynamics). Asli insani boli me shabdon ke badalne par muh aur jeebh ke movement se vocal brightness tezi se dynamically change hoti hai. Deepfake voice synthesis me formant frequency transition unnaturally static aur flat hoti hai.

1.4 Minimum-Statistics Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) Cellular Guard

CELLULAR NOISE GUARD
$$\text{SNR}_{\text{dB}} = 20 \log_{10}\left(\frac{\mu_{\text{peak\_rms}}}{\mu_{\text{noise\_floor}}}\right) \quad \text{Guard Condition: } \text{SNR} \ge 12.0\text{ dB}$$
Where As:
• $\mu_{\text{noise\_floor}}$: Mean RMS energy of bottom $20\%$ quietest frames (tracks cellular ambient noise).
• $\mu_{\text{peak\_rms}}$: Mean RMS energy of top $20\%$ loudest speech burst frames.
• Guard Action: If $\text{SNR} < 12.0\text{ dB} \implies \text{DEGRADED\_SIGNAL}$ (prevents noisy cellular call false alarms).
💡 Kyu & Kiske Liye (Why & Purpose):
Cellular Call False-Alarm Prevention. 2G/3G/4G cellular phone calls me background traffic ya weak signal ki wajah se audio quality degrade ho sakti hai. Yeh formula noise floor track karta hai taaki agar noise bohot zyada ho ($<12\text{ dB}$), toh false alert dene ke bajaye safely DEGRADED_SIGNAL mark kare.

LINK SHIELD & QR

2.1 Shannon Information Entropy $H(X)$ (DGA Botnet Defense)

DGA DETECTION
$$H(X) = -\sum_{i=1}^{n} P(x_i) \log_2 P(x_i) \quad \text{where} \quad P(x_i) = \frac{\text{count}(x_i)}{\text{length}(S)}$$
Where As:
• $S$: URL domain or path string; $n$: Count of unique characters present in string $S$.
• $P(x_i)$: Probability mass function of character symbol $x_i$ occurring in the string.
• Threshold: $H(X) \ge 3.8\text{ bits/char} \implies \text{DGA Botnet Link}$ (Algorithmic pseudorandom character soup).
• Baseline: Legitimate domains (e.g. google.com, amazon.in) exhibit low entropy $H(X) \approx 2.4 - 3.2\text{ bits/char}$.
💡 Kyu & Kiske Liye (Why & Purpose):
Algorithmic DGA (Domain Generation Algorithm) Botnet Links ko pakadne ke liye. Phishing attack bots automated pseudorandom gibberish domains (e.g. a9x8z7q1w2e3.xyz) banate hain jinki information randomness $H(X) \ge 3.8$ hoti hai.

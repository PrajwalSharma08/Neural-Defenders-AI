4 Key Features & DSP Formulas
38-Feature Acoustic Vector F ∈ R^38:
1. Mel-Scale Filterbank Formula:
m = 2595 * log10(1 + f / 700)
• m: Mel pitch, f: Frequency (Hz). 2595 & 700: Cochlear response constants. Extracts 26 MFCCs (13 Raw + 13 Del) via DCT for vocal tract geometry.
2. Spectral Centroid Formula:
C = Σ [f(k) * |X(k)|] / Σ |X(k)|
• C: Sound Brightness in Hz, |X(k)|: FFT magnitude. Detects metallic ringing in AI vocoders.
3. Zero-Crossing Rate (ZCR):
ZCR = (1/2N) * Σ |sgn(x[n]) - sgn(x[n-1])|
• N: 3200 samples (200ms). Human Fricatives: 25-35%; AI vocoders show abnormal flat ZCR.
4. Wiener Spectral Flatness:
S_f = exp( (1/K) Σ ln|X_k|^2 ) / ( (1/K) Σ |X_k|^2 )
• Ratio of Geometric to Arithmetic mean. Distinguishes white noise from pure tones.
5 Innovation & Link Guard Formulas
A. 2.5s Acoustic Variance (Breathing/Biological Pause):
σ_S = sqrt[ (1/K) * Σ (E_m - E_bar)^2 ] / (E_bar + ε)
• Tracks biological breathing silence pauses (10-18 dB SNR). Human σ_S > 0.12 vs AI σ_S < 0.04.
B. Shannon Entropy (Link Guard Heuristics):
H(X) = - Σ P(x_i) * log2 P(x_i)
• P(x_i): Char probability in URL. Legitimate H < 3.0 bits/char; DGA Bot Phishing H > 3.5.
C. Cloaked Open-Redirect & Typosquatting Metrics:
lev(inbound, brand) < 2 | target_host ≠ outer_host
• Detects typosquatting spoofs ("amazn" vs "amazon") and deep hidden forwarding targets.

KEY FEATURES & DSP FORMULAS

38-Feature Acoustic Vector F ∈ R^38:
1. Mel-Scale: m = 2595 * log10(1 + f / 700)
• m: Mel pitch, f: Frequency (Hz), 2595 & 700: Cochlear response constants. Extracts 26 MFCCs (13 Mean + 13 Std) via DCT for vocal tract geometry.
2. Spectral Centroid: C = Σ ( f(k) * |X(k)| ) / Σ |X(k)|
• C: Sound Brightness in Hz, |X(k)|: FFT magnitude. Detects metallic ringing in AI vocoders.
3. ZCR (Fricatives): ZCR = (1/2N) * Σ |sgn(x[n]) - sgn(x[n-1])|
• N: 3200 samples (200ms). Human fricatives (S, Sh) have high ZCR variance (Std>0.05); AI is flat.
4. Wiener Flatness: S_f = exp(1/K * Σ ln|X_k|^2) / (1/K * Σ|X_k|^2)
• Ratio of Geometric to Arithmetic Mean. Distinguishes white noise from pure tones.

INNOVATION & LINK GUARD FORMULAS

A. 2.5s Acoustic Variance: σ_S = sqrt( (1/M) * Σ (E_m - E_bar)^2 ) / (E_bar + ε)
• Tracks biological breathing micro-pauses (20-30 dB SPL). Human σ_S > 0.12 vs AI σ_S < 0.05.
B. Shannon Entropy: H(X) = - Σ P(x_i) * log2 P(x_i)
• P(x_i): Char probability in URL. Legitimate H ≤ 3.0 bits/char; DGA Bot Phishing H > 3.5.
C. Levenshtein Matrix: lev(inbound, brand) ≤ 2
• Detects typosquatting spoofs ('arnazon' vs 'amazon', 'sbi-kyc' vs 'sbi') across 60+ banks.
